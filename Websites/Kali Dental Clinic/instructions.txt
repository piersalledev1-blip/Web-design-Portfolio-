KALI DENTAL CLINIC website — Paystack live-mode integration notes.

The Supabase project already has two deployed Edge Functions:

1. `paystack-initialize`
- Accepts a booking ID and private tracking code.
- Validates the booking.
- Gets the authoritative payment amount from the database.
- Initializes a Paystack transaction.
- Returns the checkout authorization URL and payment reference.

2. `paystack-webhook`
- Receives Paystack webhook events.
- Validates the HMAC-SHA512 signature.
- Checks payment reference, amount and currency.
- Records successful payments and confirms appointments.
