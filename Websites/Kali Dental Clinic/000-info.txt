DENTAL BOOKING SYSTEM RESEARCH — GHANA
=====================================
Prepared: 2026-10-29
Scope: Ghanaian dental clinic websites that offer (or claim to offer) online
appointment booking. Focus on the most professional sites in Accra and other
major cities.
Method: Web search + direct inspection of public booking pages. Findings are
based on visible page content/forms. Pages that timed out or hide content
behind JavaScript are marked "not fully verified".


1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------
The Ghanaian dental market is NOT yet using true real-time scheduling. Most
"online booking" is an appointment REQUEST form that a receptionist confirms
by phone, WhatsApp, SMS or email. The dominant conversion path is
website -> form or WhatsApp -> human confirmation.

Only a few sites approach a modern self-service flow:
- Brighton Dental (3-step wizard)
- Smile Center GH (3-step wizard, price-first)
- Akcess Dental (single-page branch + procedure + calendar + time)
- Dentoc Dental (rich single form with branch, reason, hourly slots)
- myhealthGH (true marketplace: verified clinics, live slot selection, MoMo /
  card payment, receipts, reschedule/cancel)

Two distinct patterns dominate:
(A) Premium clinic brand + lead form / WhatsApp handoff.
(B) Content/SEO-led clinic + basic request form.

There is a clear gap for a well-designed system that offers real availability,
instant confirmation, mobile-money payment/deposit, reminders and
self-service rescheduling.


2. TIERED REVIEW (most professional first)
-----------------------------------------
Tier 1 — Most professional / closest to a real booking product

1) Akcess Dental — akcessdental.com/appointment
   - Modern React-style site, clean brand.
   - Single-page booking: branch selector (North Legon, Oyarifa, Ho),
     procedure selector with description + duration (e.g. checkup 60 min,
     cleaning 45 min), calendar date picker, time selection, patient details,
     optional referral, optional notes.
   - "We'll confirm your appointment within 24 hours."
   - Reset form / Submit appointment.
   - Multi-branch + multi-service handled well. Still request-based.

2) myhealthGH — myhealthgh.com
   - Health marketplace, not a single clinic. The most "system" of the set.
   - Patients choose a verified doctor, then clinic + date + time on one
     screen.
   - Payments: MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money, Visa/Mastercard via
     Paystack. Official receipts.
   - Verification: doctors licensed by Medical & Dental Council of Ghana;
     facilities licensed by HeFRA.
   - Family member profiles; book for dependants.
   - Patient dashboard with reschedule, cancel, receipts.
   - Coverage: Accra, Kumasi, Tamale, Takoradi; mobile apps "coming soon".
   - This is the reference model for a marketplace-style build.

3) Dentoc Dental Clinic — dentocdentalclinic.com
   - 6 branches (East Legon, North Legon, Ashaiman, Weija, Nkawkaw,
     Sunyani-Fiapre).
   - Strong single form: full name, phone, email, nearest branch, preferred
     date, reason (13 options incl emergency), preferred time (hourly slots
     8am-4pm), notes, honeypot spam field.
   - "We'll confirm within one business day." Inline success state with
     fallback phone number.
   - Insurance logos, 4.9-star social proof, branch directory.
   - Very complete lead-capture design.

   - Clear 3-step wizard: Select Service -> Choose Date & Time -> Personal

   - 16 services including CBCT, OPG, periapicals, emergency, orthodontics.
   - Team profiles with credentials (BDS, MDC certified, years, Invisalign

5) Smile Center GH — smilecentergh.com/book/simple

   - Price-first model: GHS prices for whitening, polishing, scaling, tooth

6) Bethel Dental Clinic — betheldentalghana.com

7) Amoah Dental — amoahdental.com.gh
   - Premium "American-standard" positioning; strong trust copy (20+ years US

3. BOOKING FLOW ARCHETYPES
--------------------------
A. WhatsApp / phone only
   CTA -> tel: or wa.me. No data capture. Common, trusted, zero build cost.

B. Basic request form
   Name, phone, email, preferred date/time, notes -> staff confirms.
   Examples: Sinolle, Dental Signature, Ningels, ProDent.

C. Rich single-page request
   Branch + service + calendar + time slot + details + notes.
   Examples: Akcess, Dentoc.

D. Multi-step wizard
   Step 1 service, Step 2 date/time, Step 3 personal details, then review.
   Examples: Brighton, Smile Center.

E. WhatsApp handoff
   Form composes a pre-filled WhatsApp message; patient sends it.
   Example: Amoah. Good for privacy and low backend cost.

F. Third-party marketplace / directory
   Clinic profile on an aggregator; booking + payment handled centrally.
   Examples: Community Dental via afridoctor, myhealthGH.

G. Instant paid booking (rare)
   Real availability + online payment + receipt + self-service changes.
   Example: myhealthGH. Almost no single clinic does this.


4. RECURRING UX / CONTENT PATTERNS
----------------------------------
Entry points
- Persistent "Book Appointment" in header and hero.
- Same CTA repeated on service pages, mid-page sections and footer.
- Floating WhatsApp button on many sites.
- Phone number click-to-call in header/footer.
- Emergency dental care given its own CTA.

Step order (when a flow exists)
1. Choose service / reason for visit.
2. Choose branch / location (multi-site clinics).
3. Choose date.
4. Choose time.
5. Enter personal details.
6. Review and submit.
7. Confirmation / "what happens next".

Form fields (most to least common)
- Full name — universal, required.
- Phone number — universal, required; primary contact key.
- Email — common; often optional.
- Preferred date — common.
- Preferred time — sometimes a free-text preference; better sites use slots.
- Service / reason — common on richer forms (10-16 options typical).
- Branch / location — required for multi-site practices.
- Message / notes — common, optional.
- "How did you hear about us?" — Omabod.
- SMS/email consent — Bethel.
- Referral — Akcess (optional).
- Honeypot field — Amoah, Dentoc.
- reCAPTCHA / CAPTCHA — Bethel, Ningels.
- Insurance details — almost never captured at booking.
- Date of birth / new-vs-returning patient — rarely captured.

Confirmation model
- "We'll call/WhatsApp you to confirm" is the norm.
- Stated response times: 2 hours (Sinolle, Amoah), 24 hours (Akcess),
  one business day (Dentoc, Total House).
- Some show a success panel; few send automated email/SMS.
- Very few offer reschedule/cancel or reminders online.
- myhealthGH is the exception: instant confirmation, receipts, dashboard.

Trust signals
- Google review quotes, star ratings and review counts.
- Before/after galleries.
- Dentist profiles with BDS/MDC/GDA credentials, years of experience and
  specialist training.
- Insurance provider logos.
- "Official dental provider" / international training claims.
- Transparent pricing or "pocket-friendly" messaging.
- Sterilisation, technology and comfort claims.
- Location map, opening hours, multiple phone lines.
- Privacy wording and medical-data warnings (Amoah is the strongest).

Content architecture
Home, About, Services (often individual pages), Team/Doctors, Gallery,
Reviews/Testimonials, Blog, FAQs, Insurance, Patient information/forms,
Contact, Book Appointment, Emergency care, Membership plans.

SEO patterns
- Location landing pages ("Best dental clinic in Accra / Osu / Ghana").
- Service + city pages (orthodontics in Accra, implants Ghana).
- Long-form blog and FAQ content.
- "15 Best Dental Clinics in Accra" listicles used for backlinks and leads.

Commercial patterns
- Membership / loyalty plans: Smile Club (Amoah), Smile Club (Smile Center),
  in-house membership plans (Bethel).
- Session packages and per-session pricing (Smile Center).
- Free mini consultations for high-value treatments (Platinum/Invisalign).
- Financing/payment plans mentioned by some (Right Dental, Bethel).


5. GHANA-SPECIFIC PATTERNS
--------------------------
- WhatsApp is the default booking and follow-up channel. Many "Book" buttons
  are wa.me links rather than forms.
- Phone remains king: click-to-call numbers in header, footer and every
  section.
- Mobile money is expected: MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money.
  Paystack is the common card/mobile-money gateway (myhealthGH).
- Multi-branch networks are common (Dentoc 6, Bethel 3, Akcess 3,
  Dental Signature 2, Total House 3).
- Saturday openings are standard; a few offer late evenings or Sunday
  emergency cover.
- Insurance is a major trust/decision factor; logos are displayed, but
  eligibility is rarely captured online.
- Emergency dental care is called out separately.
- All sites are English-only; no Twi/Ga/Ewe localisation observed.
- Mobile-first layouts dominate; most sites are lightweight brochure sites
  with forms.
- Regulatory context referenced by the most mature player: MDC (Medical and
  Dental Council) and HeFRA facility licensing.


6. WHAT IS MISSING IN THE MARKET (OPPORTUNITIES)
------------------------------------------------
- Real-time availability and instant confirmation.
- Online deposit/payment at booking (only myhealthGH does this well).
- Automated SMS/WhatsApp/email reminders and no-show reduction.
- Self-service reschedule/cancel and waitlists.
- Patient accounts / appointment history / documents.
- Insurance eligibility capture and verification at booking.
- Structured emergency triage (severity, pain level, swelling, trauma).
- Multi-language support (English + Twi/Ga/Ewe).
- Accessibility (WCAG) and low-bandwidth optimisation.
- Calendar sync (.ics) and Google/Outlook integration.
- Transparent pricing and cost estimates before the visit.
- Clear data-protection consent aligned with Ghana's Data Protection Act.
- Better validation, fewer CAPTCHAs, fewer dead links and faster pages.
- Analytics/funnel tracking on booking drop-off.


7. IMPLICATIONS FOR OUR BUILD
-----------------------------
Assumption: we are building TWO related products, e.g.
(A) a clinic-owned booking system, and
(B) a multi-clinic marketplace or clinic admin dashboard. Adjust if wrong.

Recommended patient flow (mobile-first, under 60 seconds):
1. Reason for visit (cards, not a long dropdown) with "Not sure / Emergency".
2. Branch or "nearest to me" (geolocation optional).
3. Date -> available slots only (no past dates, no closed days, buffer times).
4. Patient details: full name + Ghana phone (auto-format 0XX / +233).
   Email optional. Notes optional. Consent checkbox.
5. Review screen: service, branch, clinician, date/time, estimated price.
6. Submit -> instant confirmation (if slot is held) or "request received"
   with a clear response-time promise and WhatsApp fallback.
7. Confirmation page + SMS/WhatsApp message + .ics + reschedule link.

Minimum data model
- Clinic (branches, hours, timezone Africa/Accra, closures)
- Clinician (specialties, services, availability)
- Service (name, duration, price/GHS, deposit, category)
- Appointment (patient, clinic, clinician, service, start/end, status,
  source, notes, consent, payment status)
- Patient (name, phone, email, DOB optional, preferred channel)
- Payment (provider, amount, currency GHS, status, receipt)
- Notification log (channel, template, status)

Status model
requested -> pending_confirmation -> confirmed -> checked_in -> completed
                                      -> no_show
                                      -> cancelled / rescheduled

Key differentiators to win in Ghana
- WhatsApp-native notifications and confirmations (not just email).
- Mobile money + card via Paystack, with optional deposit.
- Real availability, instant confirmation, self-service reschedule.
- Low-friction: no account required; phone number is the identity.
- Trust: verified clinic badge (MDC/HeFRA), reviews, transparent pricing.
- Works on cheap Android phones and poor networks (small payloads, offline-
  tolerant forms, retry).

Suggested stack (pragmatic)
- Frontend: mobile-first PWA; server-rendered or lightweight SPA.
- Backend: REST/JSON API; Postgres; unique constraint on
  (clinician_id, start_time) to prevent double booking; row-level locking or
  transactional slot hold.
- Auth: phone OTP for patients; role-based accounts for staff/admin.
- Payments: Paystack (MoMo + card), webhooks for confirmation.
- Notifications: WhatsApp Business API / SMS gateway + email fallback.
- Admin: calendar day/week view, drag-to-reschedule, status board, no-show
  tracking, export.

Build order
Phase 1 (MVP): clinic profile, services, branches, request form, staff
confirmation, WhatsApp/SMS notification, basic admin list.
Phase 2: real availability + slot holds, instant confirmation, deposits,
reminders, reschedule links.
Phase 3: marketplace (multiple clinics, verified badges, search by location/
service), reviews, payouts, analytics.
