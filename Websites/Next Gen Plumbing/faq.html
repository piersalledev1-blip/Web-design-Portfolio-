FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions?
We've Got Answers.

Find answers to common questions about Next Gen's plumbing, bathroom, kitchen and home improvement services.

What services does Next Gen provide? +

Next Gen provides professional plumbing, bathroom, kitchen and home improvement services. Contact us to discuss the specific work you need.

Do you handle bathroom renovations? +

Yes. Bathroom renovation projects can include plumbing, fixtures, fittings and other improvements depending on the scope of the project.

Do you handle kitchen renovations? +

Yes. You can contact Next Gen to discuss kitchen plumbing, installations and other renovation requirements.

Do you provide plumbing installations? +

Yes. Next Gen can discuss plumbing installation requirements for new projects, renovations and existing properties.

How long does a project take? +

Project duration depends on the size, complexity and scope of the work. We'll discuss the expected timeline for your project before work begins, so you know what to expect.

Do you provide emergency plumbing services? +

Please contact Next Gen directly to discuss your plumbing emergency and determine how quickly we can assist.

Do you work on both new and existing properties? +

Yes. Next Gen can work on new construction, existing homes and renovation projects. The approach depends on the condition of the property and the scope of the work.

Can you renovate a bathroom completely? +

Yes. Bathroom projects can include plumbing, fixtures, fittings and other renovation work. We'll first discuss what you want to change and determine the scope of the project.

Do you handle kitchen plumbing? +

Yes. We can discuss kitchen plumbing requirements as part of a new installation, renovation or improvement project.

Can you replace old plumbing fixtures? +

Yes. If you need to replace taps, sinks, toilets, showers or other plumbing fixtures, contact Next Gen so we can understand what needs to be replaced.

Do you install bathroom fixtures? +

Yes. Bathroom fixture installation can be included depending on the requirements of your project.

Can you help with plumbing for a new bathroom? +

Yes. New bathroom plumbing can be discussed as part of your project. We'll need to understand the space, requirements and intended fixtures before work begins.

Do you work on kitchen and bathroom renovations together? +

Yes. If you're renovating both spaces, you can discuss the full scope with Next Gen so the plumbing and improvement work can be planned together.

How do I request a quote? +

You can contact Next Gen through WhatsApp or by phone. Tell us what you need, and we'll discuss your project and the next steps.

Can I contact you before deciding to start? +

Absolutely. You can contact Next Gen to discuss your project, ask questions and understand the next steps before making a decision.

What information should I provide when contacting you? +

It helps to provide a description of the work you need, the type of property, your location and any important details about the problem or renovation.

Can I send photos of the plumbing problem? +

Yes. Sending clear photos through WhatsApp can help explain the problem and give us a better understanding of what you're dealing with.

Do you work with homeowners? +

Yes. Homeowners can contact Next Gen about plumbing, bathroom, kitchen and home improvement projects.

Do you work with landlords and property owners? +

Yes. Property owners can contact Next Gen to discuss plumbing installations, repairs, renovations and other relevant home improvement work.

What areas do you serve? +

Service availability depends on the location and type of project. Contact Next Gen directly to confirm whether your project is within our service area.

Can you work on older plumbing systems? +

Older plumbing systems can be assessed to determine what work is required. Contact Next Gen with details about the existing system and the issue you're experiencing.

What happens after I contact Next Gen? +

We'll discuss your needs, gather the important project details and determine the appropriate next step for your project.

Do you explain the work before starting? +

Yes. Before work begins, we'll discuss the project, the work involved and what needs to be done so you have a clear understanding of the process.

How can I contact Next Gen? +

You can call Next Gen on +233 20 440 7250 or start a conversation through WhatsApp.
