How it works

A clear process.
From idea to launch.

From the first conversation to the moment your website goes live, everything has a clear stage. You provide the information and direction — I handle the design, development and technical setup.

01

Discover

We learn about your business, your goals, your customers, and what you want the website to accomplish.

02

Onboarding

You provide the essentials we need to build the website — your business name, services, phone number, email, address, photos, videos, logo and other relevant information. I’ll also ask a few questions to understand your business and how you want it presented.

03

Plan

We organize everything into the right website structure and determine the content, sections and direction.

04

Design & Build

The website is designed and developed around your business, with the structure, visuals and user experience working together as one complete experience.

05

Review the Mockup

You receive the first version to review. You can point out anything you want changed before the final version is prepared.

06

Final Version

After your feedback on the mockup, I make the agreed changes and prepare the final version of the website.

07

Final Review

You get to review the final version one last time. Once you’ve approved it, we’re ready for launch.

08

Launch & Ownership

I handle the technical setup and launch the website for you, so you don’t have to worry about the technical side.
